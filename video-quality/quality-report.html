How this test works: Each bot instance is a headless Playwright browser that opens the vehicle's live WebRTC stream URL, plays back the video for 60 seconds, and records network statistics (via RTCPeerConnection.getStats()) every second. Metrics like bitrate, jitter, latency (RTT), frame rate, frames dropped, freezes and packet loss are aggregated across the 60-second window to produce a quality score and status badge for each vehicle.

🔢 What Do "4 Passed / 0 Failed" Mean?
✔
Passed (Stream Connection)

The bot successfully connected to the WebRTC stream URL and received measurable video data during the 60-second test window. Note: this does NOT mean the video quality was acceptable — a stream can pass connection but still receive a WARNING or FAILED quality badge based on metrics.

✖
Failed (Stream Connection)

The bot could not connect to the WebRTC stream at all, or the stream timed out before delivering any video frames. This is a hard connection-level failure — no quality data was collected.

↺
Flaky (Intermittent Connection)

The stream connected on retry but failed at least once. Indicates an unstable or unreliable connection to the vehicle stream endpoint.

🏷️ Per-Vehicle Quality Badges (Score-Based)
✔
GOOD (Score ≥ 80 / 100)

All critical metrics are within acceptable thresholds. The stream is performing well with no significant quality concerns.

⚠
WARNING (Score 60–79 / 100)

One or more metrics are outside the ideal range but have not yet crossed the critical failure threshold. The stream is watchable but degraded — e.g. elevated latency, occasional freezes, or slow startup. Needs monitoring.

✖
FAILED (Score < 60 / 100)

One or more metrics have crossed a critical threshold — e.g. excessive video freezes (> 15% of stream time), very high jitter (> 50 ms), or critical startup delays. The stream quality is unacceptable for real-world use.

📊 How the Quality Score (x/100) Is Calculated:
Each metric is scored on a 0–100 scale based on the thresholds above. The weighted composite score emphasises the most user-visible metrics: Freezes / Stutters (30%), Frame Rate (20%), Jitter (15%), Latency (15%), Startup Time (10%), Bitrate (5%), Packet Loss (5%). A score of 80+ = Good, 60–79 = Warning, < 60 = Failed.
